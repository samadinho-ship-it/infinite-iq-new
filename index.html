GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

A few useful details before you send a project enquiry.

How do I get a quote?
Choose a package or send a general enquiry. Tell us what you need, and we'll discuss the project scope before providing a quote.
Do I need to know exactly what I want?
No. You can describe your business and your main goals. The free Business Website Planner and Project Brief Builder can also help you organise your starting ideas.
Will my website work on mobile phones?
Mobile-friendly layouts are included in the website packages. The final design is planned for phones as well as larger screens.
Are domain names and hosting included?
Not automatically. Domain names, hosting and some third-party services may have separate costs. These details should be agreed before the project starts.
Does sending an enquiry mean I have to pay?
No. Sending an enquiry is just the first step in discussing your idea. Scope, pricing and arrangements are discussed before any work begins.
Are the INFINITE IQ tools free?
The Business Website Planner, Project Brief Builder and Project Dashboard are free browser-based tools. They do not require a paid AI subscription or API. Dashboard data stays in the browser on the device where it was entered.
What is the Client Portal?
The Client Portal is a project information preview where visitors can explore a project overview, tasks and updates. The current version is a demo and does not provide real client accounts or live shared project updates.
